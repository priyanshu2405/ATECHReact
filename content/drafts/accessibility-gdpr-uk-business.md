## Proposed H2 Structure & Key Points

### H1: Website Accessibility and GDPR Basics for UK Business Websites

### H2: 1. Why Accessibility and Privacy Matter for UK Businesses
- The Equality Act 2010 and how it applies to digital services and consumer websites in the UK.
- The UK Data Protection Act 2018 (UK GDPR) and Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) enforcement.
- Business benefits: accessible sites rank higher on search engines, load faster on mobile devices, and convert a wider demographic.

### H2: 2. Building with WCAG 2.1 AA Accessibility in Mind
- Semantic HTML landmarks: using `<main>`, `<nav>`, `<header>`, and proper heading hierarchies (`<h1>` to `<h3>`).
- Keyboard navigability: ensuring all links, buttons, and form inputs are reachable via Tab and Enter with visible focus indicators.
- Color contrast ratios: maintaining a minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for normal text against dark or light backgrounds.
- Image alternative text: descriptive `alt` tags that explain context for screen-reader users, rather than keyword stuffing.

### H2: 3. Essential UK GDPR Foundations for Any Commercial Website
- Transparent Cookie Consent: why pre-ticked analytics boxes are non-compliant, and how Google Consent Mode v2 works.
- Contact Forms & Consent: why you must provide an unticked consent checkbox linking directly to your Privacy Policy.
- Data minimization: only collecting the information you actually need to provide the service or quote.
- SSL/TLS Encryption: enforcing HTTPS and modern security headers (HSTS, Content Security Policy).

### H2: 4. Common Technical Mistakes That Flag ICO Scrutiny
- Third-party tracking scripts firing before visitor consent is given.
- Unencrypted email notification scripts that expose customer personal information.
- Vague privacy policies copied from foreign websites with inapplicable legal terms.

### H2: 5. A 10-Point Technical Checklist for UK Website Owners
- Point 1: SSL certificate active with automatic HTTPS redirect.
- Point 2: Clear cookie consent banner integrated with Tag Manager.
- Point 3: Descriptive link anchor text (no bare "click here" or unlabelled icons).
- Point 4: Fully responsive layouts tested at 375px mobile width.
- Point 5: Explicit consent checkbox on lead capture forms.
- Point 6: Dedicated Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages linked in footer.
- Point 7: One clear H1 heading per page with logical H2/H3 subheadings.
- Point 8: Alt tags on all non-decorative imagery.
- Point 9: Honeypot spam protection without disruptive CAPTCHAs.
- Point 10: Clear business identification (company name, registered address, direct contact email).
