### H2: 1. Why UK Businesses Look Offshore (And Where Most Go Wrong)

### H2: 2. Ensuring Time-Zone Alignment with the UK Working Day
- Why a 4-hour overlap (2:00 PM – 6:00 PM UK time, GMT/BST) is ideal for sprint standups and rapid Slack communication.
- Asynchronous communication best practices: using Loom video walkthroughs and structured Jira/Trello boards to keep progress uninterrupted during UK morning hours.

### H2: 3. Legal and Intellectual Property Protections Under UK Law
- Executing bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) before sharing architecture specifications.
- Ensuring full intellectual property (IP) assignment upon milestone payment—no proprietary agency licensing traps.
- Source code escrow and direct Git repository access from day one.

### H2: 4. UK GDPR and Data Privacy Considerations
- Building with UK Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR principles in mind.
- Data minimization: avoiding real customer data in development/staging environments.
- Securing API credentials, encrypted database storage, and secure hosting.

### H2: 5. How to Structure Milestone Contracts and Currency Payments
- Never pay 100% upfront: why fixed-milestone contracts (Discovery, Prototype, Core API, UAT, Launch) protect both parties.
- Paying directly in British Pounds (GBP) to avoid currency volatility and surprise conversion charges.
- The importance of a 30-day post-launch warranty window in the contract.

### H2: 6. Checklist: 7 Questions to Ask Before Signing with an Offshore Team
- Question 1: Who will write my code—senior architects or trainees?
- Question 2: Can we interview the lead developer directly?
- Question 3: How often do you deliver demo-ready working software?
- Question 4: Do we own 100% of the repository from day one?
- Question 5: What is your time overlap with London time?
- Question 6: What payment gateways and UK integrations have you built?
- Question 7: Will you sign our standard mutual NDA?
