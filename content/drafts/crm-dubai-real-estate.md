## Proposed H2 Structure & Key Points

### H1: Choosing a CRM for a Dubai Real Estate Business: Custom vs Off-the-Shelf

### H2: 1. The Unique Demands of the Dubai Real Estate Market
- Fast-moving lead cycles: why leads from Property Finder, Bayut, and Facebook Ads must be distributed to agents within seconds.
- Multi-currency transactions: dealing with AED, USD, GBP, EUR, and crypto-to-fiat tracking.
- Commission tiering: split commissions between listing agents, buyer agents, and external broker partners.

### H2: 2. Where Off-the-Shelf CRMs Fall Short in the UAE
- Ballooning per-user monthly subscription fees as brokerage agent headcount fluctuates.
- Weak or non-native WhatsApp integration: off-the-shelf platforms often require clunky third-party middleware for chat history.
- Rigid pipeline structures that do not match off-plan developer booking workflows or secondary market conveyancing stages.

### H2: 3. Essential Features for a Custom UAE Real Estate CRM
- Instant WhatsApp Business API lead capture, automated qualification bot, and direct assignment to available agents.
- Portal lead synchronization (APIs for Bayut, Property Finder, Dubizzle, and Meta Lead Ads).
- Bilingual Arabic/English interface and document generation (tenancy contracts, sales agreements, receipts).
- UAE VAT calculation (5%) and Federal Tax Authority (FTA) complaint invoicing modules.

### H2: 4. Total Cost of Ownership: 3-Year Comparison
- Subscription model (50 agents on $100/seat/month CRM) vs fixed-price custom build.
- Database and IP ownership: why brokerages must own their proprietary client lists and investor databases.
- Maintenance requirements and server hosting on regional Middle East cloud instances.

### H2: 5. How to Plan a Custom CRM in Manageable Milestones
- Phase 1: Lead ingestion, deduplication, and WhatsApp routing.
- Phase 2: Property listing catalogue and unit availability matrix.
- Phase 3: Contract generation, VAT invoices, and agent commission calculations.
- Phase 4: Mobile companion app for agents on site visits.
